Predicting Player Engagement and Identifying At-Risk Players on Steam

Negative reviews do not necessarily mean players will stop playing. We combined player behavior with NLP-derived complaint signals to predict observed post-review engagement and identify which negative reviewers may require different product or support responses.

Key Results

2023 Test ROC-AUC
0.824
Observed engagement in the top predicted-engagement decile
88.7%
Lift vs. random selection
2.38×

LightGBM with structured and NLP features performed best. Complaint signals added predictive value beyond structured player features, while the final model maintained strong ranking performance on newer 2023 reviews.

The engagement target is derived from historical playtime snapshots and should not be interpreted as confirmed activity exactly 30 days after a review.

The model also showed calibration drift on newer data, indicating that periodic recalibration would be necessary in deployment.

These profiles help distinguish players who may require different product or support responses. They are relative engagement profiles, not confirmed churn labels.
